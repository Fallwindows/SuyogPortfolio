Selected work

Isaac Sim · ROS 2 · SLAM

Grocery Aisle RGB + LiDAR Simulation

A layered harness for a grocery-store sensor rig. Isaac Sim renders the aisle and publishes RGB, RTX LiDAR, TF and clock over the ROS 2 bridge, while RTAB-Map runs odometry and SLAM against a separate ground-truth stream. A localhost dashboard streams camera, point clouds, maps and evaluation metrics live.

  • Isaac Sim 6.1
  • ROS 2 Jazzy
  • RTAB-Map
  • Python
  • USD

metaopt: Acoustic Metamaterial Optimizer

A geometry optimizer for 3D-printed acoustic absorbers. It searches unit-cell geometries for target absorption across a frequency band and exports printable designs.
